Hao Yue is an Associate Teaching Professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at the University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC), where he also serves as the Head of Education Programs at the UCSC Generative AI Center. His research is at the intersection of Generative AI and Computing Education. He has secured over $6M in funding from the National Science Foundation and other agencies to develop equitable computing curricula and student success initiatives.

A recognized leader in the field, he serves on the steering committee of the $15M California Educator Workforce Investment Grant. Dr. Yue earned his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of Florida in 2015. He has authored over 60 peer-reviewed papers and is a three-time recipient of IEEE Best Paper Awards in 2017, 2018, and 2019.

His current mission is to leverage AI-driven innovation to build inclusive, scalable computing programs that empower the next generation of diverse tech talent.
